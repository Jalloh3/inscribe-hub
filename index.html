Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A short essay or a set of social graphics can often be turned around in 1–3 days; a full dissertation chapter or a survey-based analysis usually takes 1–2 weeks.

How do I pay for a service?

We accept mobile money (Orange Money, Afrimoney) and bank transfer, usually split into a deposit to begin and a balance on delivery.

Do you work with clients outside Freetown?

Yes — writing, data, design, and scholarship/Umrah guidance are all delivered online, so location isn't a barrier.
